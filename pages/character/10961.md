# New Stages and Areas
## USJ
USJ is the very first stage added to My Hero Ultra Rumble. It features 7 unique areas, these being: Fire Zone, Typhoon Zone, Collapse Zone, Landslide Zone, Mountain Zone, Aquatic Zone and Plaza.<br>
USJ is characterized by its ample open spaces with little cover besides Fire and Typhoon Zone, and with the unique aspect of having two zones that can "change" layouts through the destruction of destructible buildings, which greatly change the area, especially in the case of Collapse Zone.

### Collapse Zone
Collapse Zone is a peculiar area with abundant buildings, which are all destructible. This area is interesting because its destructible buildings paint two difference scenes between early game Collapse Zone and its late game counterpart, as initially it will have decent cover, but as time passes, chances are the buildings will take damage from attacks and eventually break, making the area progressively more open. The buildings in this area can release civilians upon being broken, so it's an okay option if Landslide Zone isn't comfortably accessible, though, all huge buildings in Collapse Zone have 600 HP.

# New Gamemodes
Season 1 came out with 3 gamemodes: 3 Player Team Battle, Training Mode and Tutorial Mode.

## 3 Player Team Battle
3 Player Team Battle is a mode where 8 teams of 3 players each fight in a huge circular stage, and the last team standing wins. It features mechanisms such as the Poison Mist, Item Boxes, Civilians and more. This mode is currently used by Unranked Matches, Ranked Matches and CPU Matches.

### Unranked Match
This sub-mode takes the mechanics previously mentioned and applies them as specified. Unranked has no skill based matchmaking. Your performance in this sub-mode is not reflected in your profile.

### Ranked Match
This sub-mode adds a little twist to the mode by implementing a skill based matchmaking and ranks to the mode. Your performance in these matches decide how many points you obtain, and if they surpass the entry cost of the match, you will keep rising. Ranked has 4 ranks: Amateur, Beginner, Pro and Ace, and excluding Ace, all ranks have sub-ranks, such as Pro 1, Pro 2, Pro 3... You can be demoted to a lower sub-rank, but you can never be demoted to a lower standard rank. Your performance in this sub-mode is reflected in your profile.

### CPU Battle
This sub-mode is practically identical to Unranked Match, except all enemy teams are bots controlled by the CPU. There are 3 types of CPU Battle sub-modes you can choose from: Easy, Medium and Hard, each changing how competent the bots are.

## Training Mode
Training Mode is a gamemode that allows players to test characters, techs and combos thanks to its special menu, which allows players to set up health regeneration, fast cooldowns, bot behavior and even switch characters. Gimmicks such as the Poison Mist or Item Boxes don't appear in this mode, but you can find Item Sets featuring every useful item such as recovery items, level up items and even buffing items.

## Tutorial Mode
Tutorial Mode is the first gamemode every player is directed to when starting the game for the first time. This mode locks the player as Izuku Midoriya and guides them through All Might pop ups and instructions through a tutorial that showcases most of the game's mechanics. After most of the tutorial is complete, the player will face teams of bots, however there will be less than in CPU Battle. Losing a match in Tutorial Mode does not prevent you from continuing your Ultra Rumble experience.

# New Features
## InGame
### Supply Items
Supply Items are any non-character objects the player can interact with the interact button, which mostly encompasses Item Boxes, Backpacks and Items.

### Civilians
Civilians are special characters you can find around the map, either by finding them naturally, or by destroying certain destructible objects. There are 3 types of Civilians: Green-Clothed, Blue-Clothed and Kota.<br>
Green-Clothed Civilians will drop an Incomplete Revive Card and an Team HP Recovery Kit, while Blue-Clothed Civilians will drop a Team GP Recovery Kit instead of an HP one. Kota on the other hand is more special, as only one exists in every match, and will always spawn in one of the locations it is programmed to spawn in. Kota will drop 3 Team Enhancement Kits upon being rescued.

### Destructible Objects
Many objects across the map are destructible, which by itself does nothing besides making access to some item boxes easier. Some buildings, particularily the ones in Collapse Zone, Landslide Zone, and the two in Aquatic Zone can be destroyed, and doing so has a chance of releasing a Civilian.

### Poison Mist
The Poison Mist is a gimmick that surrounds the stage in a damaging mist over time. This mist does consistent continuous damage, which in most cases increases once a moving mist halts. With enough recovery items, the player can remain for extended periods of time in the mist, however, characters with lower HP pools struggle to survive for extended amounts of time due to how poison damage is flat instead of a percentage. Poison Mist damage ignores GP and targets HP only.<br>
When enough time has passed (around the 16 minutes and 30 seconds mark), the mist will engulf the whole map, leaving no safe zone behind and forcing players to take high damage until only one team survives, concluding the match. Theoretically, with the right setup it's possible to survive for quite an extended amount of time after the safe zone closes.
