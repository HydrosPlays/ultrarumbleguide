This is My Hero Ultra Rumble's very first season, released in September 28th 2023, and lasted until November 23rd 2023, culminating in a duration of 57 days. 

# New Battle Styles
The game launched with 19 battle styles: 12 from the Closed Beta, 3 from the Open Beta, and 4 which came with the game's launch, out of which 1 released mid-season, that being Shota Aizawa on October 24th 2023.

Izuku Midoriya is an Assault-class character with 300 HP, and is one of the 6 characters unlocked by default at the start of the game. His kit is as follows:<br>

**Alpha Skill: Delaware Smash Air Force**<br>
Shoot a projectile forward that does damage on impact. When crouching, spend all charges in one stronger shot that has much higher range.<br>

# New Stages and Areas
## USJ
USJ is the very first stage added to My Hero Ultra Rumble. It features 7 unique areas, these being: Fire Zone, Typhoon Zone, Collapse Zone, Landslide Zone, Mountain Zone, Aquatic Zone and Plaza.<br>
USJ is characterized by its ample open spaces with little cover besides Fire and Typhoon Zone, and with the unique aspect of having two zones that can "change" layouts through the destruction of destructible buildings, which greatly change the area, especially in the case of Collapse Zone.

# New Gamemodes
Season 1 came out with 3 gamemodes: 3 Player Team Battle, Training Mode and Tutorial Mode.

## 3-Player Team Battle
3 Player Team Battle is a mode where 8 teams of 3 players each fight in a huge circular stage, and the last team standing wins. It features mechanisms such as the Poison Mist, Item Boxes, Civilians and more. This mode is currently used by Unranked Matches, Ranked Matches and CPU Matches.

### Unranked Match
This sub-mode takes the mechanics previously mentioned and applies them as specified. Unranked has no skill based matchmaking. Your performance in this sub-mode is not reflected in your profile.
<p align="left">
    <img style="border-radius: 15px;" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HydrosPlays/ultrarumbleguide/refs/heads/main/images/button-unranked.png" width="50%"/>
</p>

### Ranked Match
This sub-mode adds a little twist to the mode by implementing a skill based matchmaking and ranks to the mode. Your performance in these matches decide how many points you obtain, and if they surpass the entry cost of the match, you will keep rising. You can be demoted to a lower sub-rank, but you can never be demoted to a lower standard rank. Your performance in this sub-mode is reflected in your profile.

### Poison Mist
The Poison Mist is a gimmick that surrounds the stage in a damaging mist over time. This mist does consistent continuous damage, which in most cases increases once a moving mist halts. With enough recovery items, the player can remain for extended periods of time in the mist, however, characters with lower HP pools struggle to survive for extended amounts of time due to how poison damage is flat instead of a percentage. Poison Mist damage ignores GP and targets HP only.<br>
When enough time has passed (around the 16 minutes and 30 seconds mark), the mist will engulf the whole map, leaving no safe zone behind and forcing players to take high damage until only one team survives, concluding the match. Theoretically, with the right setup it's possible to survive for quite an extended amount of time after the safe zone closes.

### Plus Ultra
Plus Ultra/Plus Chaos is a system in which the character fills a blue bar at the bottom center of the screen by being hit, by having a teammate die, or simply by waiting. When the Plus Ultra gauge is full, players can activate this state, which will grant them a 20% DMG Boost relative to level 9 skills, a reload speed boost, full GP restoration, and will have the the properties of level 9 skills, all of this for 15 seconds. Plus Ultra can be triggered even if the character cannot move due to stun or other hindrances, which makes it useful to break out of a difficult situation, and also pushes nearby enemies back. Plus Ultra cannot be used while drinking or while DOWNd.

### Revive
Players can be revived from a KO if their teammates can get the player's badge left at their KO location, and a full Revive Card (the pink ones), which can be obtained from Large Item Boxes, or by collecting 3 incomplete Revive Cards (the gray ones). Reviving a teammate takes a few seconds and creates a beam of light enemies can see to try and interrumpt the revive.

This term is also used for abilities that allow DOWNd players to get up, such as Ibara's special action.

### Inventory
Players all start with a bagless a 6-slot inventory. Inventory space can be increased by picking up Small, Medium or Large backpacks. The inventory also supports 2 ability slots to equip ability cards. The player can also use the aim input on their skill slots to the right to ask teammates for alpha, beta or gamma Ability Cards.

Players have an interface indicating the amount of KOs, spectators, and likes received in real time during the match. <br>
When a match ends, players can see their score, which is determined on performance such as KOs inflicted, damage dealt, placement, etc.

## OutGame
OutGame features are those present outside playable scenarios such as everything in the Main Menu.

### Character Customization
Characters have 4 types of customization: Costumes, Emotes, Voicelines and Emblems.<br>
Costumes are the attire characters wear, which come with 5 color variations (Hero Style/Villain Style, Heat, Combat, Fancy, Dangerous) that the player can unlock through different means. Costumes come in 4 qualities:

- C (Common): For the player's default costume. This rarity is represented by a red frame.
- R (Rare): For color variations of non-PUR costumes. This rarity is represented by a silver frame.
- SR (Super Rare): For the default variation of non-PUR costumes, excluding the default costume's. This rarity is represented by a gold frame.
- PUR (Plus Ultra Rare): For the highest quality costumes, rarity that applies to both the PUR costume in question and its color variation. This rarity is represented by a rainbow frame.

<br>
PUR costumes have the ability to carry auras, which are a special cosmetic of costumes that are obtained through copies of the costumes. Each copy of the same costume unlocks a higher aura level, each aura level is bigger and has a different color, and players can switch between one or the other after unlocking them, the colors in unlock order are: Blue, Green, Yellow, Red, and White Rainbowish (For Heroes) or Purple (For Villains).<br>
<br>
Emotes are actions you can perform that are accompanied by a voiceline, and can be used to socialize. Common emote actions include greetings, agreeing, disagreeing, clapping, etc. Unlike costumes, emotes only seem to come in two rarities: Common (red frame) and Super Rare (gold frame). A character can only equip 8 emotes into their emote wheel. Default emotes can be overwritten by the player if so they wish.<br>
<br>
Voicelines are another equipable object that can be used to communicate with teammates mid-match, such as by communicating positivity with positive voicelines, or apologizing after one's mistake or regret.<br>
<br>
Emblems are little badges players can equip so that they're visible in their profile. These emblems are obtained by completing special missions.<br>

### Ranking
The ranking is a system where players and agencies are ranked based on a given variable. There are 4 rankings: Billboard, MVP, Agency Ranking and Elite Ranking.
- The Billboard Chart ranks players based on how many likes they've received.
- The MVP Ranking ranks players based on the amount of rank points they've kept from playing ranked matches.
- Agency Ranking ranks agencies based on how many contribution points its players have gathered, which are obtained by playing matches.
- Elite Ranking ranks agencies based on the score of the 10 highest score players in the agency.

### Agency System
Agencies are a feature that attempts to mimic the concept of "clans" in other games. You can join an agency from the default list, or by using the search features.<br>
<br>
Agencies have 3 facilities that members can level up to increase Gold, EXP or Contribution Point gains depending on which one they decide to level. Leveling facilities will also raise the Agency's level, as Agency level is determined by the sum of the level of the 3 facilities divided by 3 (rounded down). Every agency level up grants the agency an EXP Boost that can be used by the agency's leader or co-leader whenever desired. Certain levels also raise the amount of player slots the agency has, allowing to store more players. Agencies are also relevant for the Agency Ranking and the Elite Ranking.
