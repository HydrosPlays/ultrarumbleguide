Tokoyami is a jack-of-all-trades that constantly switches between a normal form and a stance that significantly empowers his mobility and melee power. He excels at enemy lockdown, mobility, and general utility.
<br>

His base kit covers his basic needs containing a slow-moving but piercing projectile Alpha, a small but long-lasting lockdown AoE Beta, and a weak but versatile shield Gamma.
Tokoyami can leap high into the air, and he can glide at will while healing. He can also protect himself while performing actions, such as healing, reviving, and rescuing civilians.
<br>

What sets Tokoyami over the edge is his Special Action stance, Black Ankh. When in Black Ankh, Tokoyami becomes significantly stronger. He moves much faster, his melees are greatly enhanced, and he can leap an additional time.
This is not without a significant downside. If Tokoyami does not manage his Black Ankh resource well, he will be left vulnerable for a long period of time, and has to rely on his basic kit.
<br>

Play Tokoyami if you enjoy managing a powerful but punishing stance resource, constantly switching between stances, locking down enemies, having good mobility, and having weak but versatile tools.

<h2 id="Alpha">Alpha Skill - Ragnarok</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch011/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch011_Unique1.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Tokoyami's Alpha sends out Dark Shadow in a straight line to attack opponents. Dark Shadow can hit multiple opponents and even travel up walls. The main weaknesses of this move is that it deals below-average damage, is fairly slow, and only has three uses. While the cooldown is forgiving, every shot needs to count.

<h2 id="Beta">Beta Skill - Spiral Shadow Born of the Gloaming</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch011/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch011_Unique2.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Tokoyami's Beta sends out Dark Shadow to a location, who then proceeds to attack and stagger opponents inside the circle. This move has many uses. It can be used to interrupt an opponent, cover for your teammates, or cover an escape route. The move has a fairly long duration which can limit opponent’s wake up options and can punish aggressive players. The main weakness of this move is that it causes high down power which can limit potential combo routes for yourself or teammates.

<h2 id="Gamma"> Gamma Skill - Jet-Black Shadow Shield</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch011/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch011_Unique3.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Tokoyami's Gamma commands Dark Shadow to protect Tokoyami with a small, weak shield with a large amount of utility. It reloads extremely slow, so it's best to completely break it when at low percentage. Keep in mind the shield is very visible to enemies. If they didn't already know where you are, then with shield they will. It's great at blocking physical attacks, but will crumble versus beamer characters, ie. Deku.

<p>
    The interactions between skills and Tokoyami's gamma are as follows:<br>
    ✅ Means "Blocks and Staggers"<br>
    🟡 Means "Blocks Only"<br>
    ❌ Means "Ignores"<br>
    ➖ Means "Not Applicable"<br><br>

    * Denotes skills that can go through Beta at close ranges<br>
    ** Denotes skills that need to be blocked at specific angles<br>

</p>

<h2 id="Special_Action">Special Action - Black Ankh / Twin Wings of the Black Arm</h2>

<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch011/GUI/Skill/T_ui_SpecialSkill_Ch011.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Tokoyami's special action allows him to enhance his melee attacks and mobility. Black Ankh is a stance that enhances Tokoyami's general movement and melee attacks. Twin Wings of the Black Arm allows him to leap high into the sky and glide in the air.

<h3 id="Black_Ankh">Black Ankh</h3>

Dark Shadow covers Tokoyami, greatly increasing his movement speed, melee attack power, and melee tracking. Tokoyami's Air Melee and Ground Melee fire out large projectiles, but these will not activate melee special tunings and will not pierce enemies. All melee attacks will gain an increase in down power, meaning Tokoyami sacrifices combos for the ability to heavily pressure opponents by constantly knocking them down. During Black Ankh, Tokoyami's leap costs 40% of his Special Action meter instead of 50% and leap even higher.

<h3 id="Twin_Wings_of_the_Black_Arm">Twin Wings of the Black Arm</h3>

Tokoyami uses Dark Shadow to leap high into the air, allowing him to attack from above or escape a fight. The ability can be used midair, allowing Tokoyami to ascend higher.

By pressing the sprint button while in the air, Tokoyami will glide through the use of Dark Shadow. This ability is a considered passive and is tied to Black Ankh, but does not need or consume Special Action meter. Clever use of your abilities or the environment will allow you to travel great distances. Tokoyami can use items while gliding, allowing you to heal yourself while you escape a fight, or heal your team from the safety of the skies.

<h2 id="Basic_Attacks">Basic Attacks</h2>

<h3 id="Ground_Melee">Ground Melee</h3>

A basic 4-hit string with fair tracking and decent damage. The 1st melee is a double hit whirlwind. The 2nd melee is a rising backwards swipe. The 3rd melee is a dive that falls at an approximate ~45 Degree angle until it hits the ground.

An aerial melee with serviceable tracking, but has low downpower of 100 and has a ground bounce property on HP. Air melee provides the main structure of Tokoyami Normal Form Combos and is amazing melee to use during team combos.

<h2 id="Black_Ankh_Attacks">Black Ankh Attacks</h3>

Black Ankh Melees are the same as normal forms, but have extra properties tied to them.

<h3 id="Black_Ankh_Ground_Melee">Black Ankh Ground Melee</h3>

A high-damaging 4-hit string combo.

On the 1st melee attack, Tokoyami throws out an extremely large projectile that does decent damage. The projectile is so large that it can hit opponents past ledges and around corners. It also has a very low down power, making it a good choice for tagging on extra damage during a teammate's combo. Keep in mind, the projectile does not pierce enemies, it merely stops at the first one struck.

The 2nd melee attack is exactly the same as the Normal form except with slightly less down power.

The 3rd melee attack has a shockwave that performs a hard knockdown. The shockwave is useful for salvaging the string if the initial 2 melees did not land.

<h3 id="Black_Ankh_Air_Melee">Black Ankh Air Melee</h3>

An high damage aerial claw slash that has a projectile. This air melee has incredible tracking and high down power.

Compared to the Black Ankh Ground Melee projectile, it does more damage and goes farther, but has a higher downpower and smaller hit box. The projectile's main draw is that you can stay mobile while firing a projectile.

<h2 id="Leveling_Paths">Leveling Paths</h2>

Tokoyami is not very level dependent due to his basic kit performing below average and Black Ankh providing a lot of damage and utility. Generally, Gamma is leveled last as it reloads very slow.
These level paths are merely a suggestion and not a rule.

<h2 id="Techs_&_Strategy">Techs & Strategy</h2>

<h3 id="Black_Ankh_Meter_Management">Black Ankh Meter Management</h3>

Black Ankh is Tokoyami's most important resource. The penalty cooldown for using all of Black Ankh meter is 30 seconds. Being without Special Action puts Tokoyami in a very bad situation. His other skills often times will not be enough to cover for him during that down period. Therefore, you should usually untransform to conserve meter when it's at a low percentage or when there are no engagements. To do this, you can manually untransform or use Alpha, Beta, or Gamma.

In a life or death situation, it's okay to use all of your Black Ankh meter. It's better to live than to save a meter you can't use when dead.

A key note is that Black Ankh will not start recharging while in the air. You need to either touch the ground, cling on to a wall, or wall run to start the recharge.

<h3 id="Covering_Wake-Ups">Covering wake-ups</h3>

Tokoyami has excellent tools at catching an opponent on their wake-up from a Knockdown.

In normal form, Tokoyami's Beta is the best option as it's the only move that staggers aside from melees. It is also very active and at worst can trade with an enemy.

In Black Ankh, Tokoyami's Air Melee has excellent tracking and can chain knockdown enemies. It's best when used against aggressive players or players that want to use their movement after a tech.
Black Ankh's Ground Melee is decent at catching wake-ups. BA Melee 1 has good tracking and BA Melee 3 has a large shockwave that will often catch opponents that aren't paying attention. It provides the most damage out of all the wake-up options, but BA Melee 1 can easily be rolled.

<h3 id="Shield_Flight">Shield Flight</h3>

Tokoyami can fly with his shield out. This can be useful to protect yourself while either gaining distance on an enemy or running away from one. Do remember, Tokoyami's shield is very weak, so this will stall only for a little bit.
During the start-up of jump or falling off a ledge, you can use both Tokoyami's shield and flight. This requires quick consecutive button presses.

The order of inputs for jumping:
Jump > (During Jump Start-up) Gamma > Flight

The order of inputs for walking off a ledge:
Gamma > (Fall off Ledge) > Flight

Keep in mind, this cannot be done mid-air or off a walljump. You must be either be starting a jump or falling off a ledge.

You can use it for creative ways to traverse the map, such as to juke enemies, or to go to alternative entrances.
