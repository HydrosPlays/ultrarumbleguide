# Character Skills
- Some abilities were slightly changed in terms of how their ammo works. For example, in CBT-1, All Might's gamma had 2 charges, which you could hold to spin for longer. However, in CBT-2, they made it so that his gamma has 50 ammo, where 50 isn't the amount of times he can use the skill, but instead, the "duration". This is basically the primitive form of percentage based skills, since in the game's code percentage based skills are just abilities with really high ammo count.
- Compared to CBT-1, All Might's jump special action now allows him to jump much higher, but it has a cooldown now.

# General Character Features
- The time it takes for a DOWNd character to get up was nerfed from 15s to 20s.

# Battle Royale Gameplay and Systems
- Added Gold Item Boxes.
- Added a Spectator and Likes counter to the top left corner.

# GUI, UI, VFX and Models/Textures
- Pre-Lobby UI has been improved
- Many characters such as Toga, Shigaraki, Dabi and Deku now display their Special Action sprite to the left of their 3 skills, as back in CBT-1 only abilities that could actively be used at any point like Shoto's or Ochako's showed up.
- Damage numbers now appear and disappear more smoothly.
- Some 3D models in USJ were adjusted, such as the tall metallic structure with a slider in Aquatic Zone.
- Many character VFX were improved.
