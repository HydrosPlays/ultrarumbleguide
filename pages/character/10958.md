## Extra Stats

- The overall most played Battle Style was Izuku Midoriya (Full Bullet), followed by Hawks (Default), with these battle styles being present over half of the teams for all 4 matches.
- Meliora's final match provided the highest KO count and team KO count in the tournament! With a total of 14 Team KOs in Match 4.
- The Assault and Technical classes were pretty much ignored, as players prefered Strike and Rapid (though this is mostly due to Hawks and Full Bullet)
- At the end of the tournament, the trailer for Season 8 was shown, revealing Nejire Hado (Default) and Eijiro Kirishima (Red Drive) as the next battle styles coming to My Hero Ultra Rumble, saying goodbye to what for many was one of the worst seasons in My Hero Ultra Rumble balancing-wise, all due to what in current times we refer to as "Prime Hawks".
