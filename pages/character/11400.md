She boasts strong punishment for enemy mistakes, absurd burst area of effect damage, and team utility. However, she is hampered by high recovery on her skills, a large hitbox, and slow mobility.
        </p>

            Star's is a character that rewards strong aim and macro (Big-Picture decision making). She requires a slower and more methodical playstyle than some characters, but has some room to bend the rules.
        </p>
        <p>
            Star can group up enemies to allow for teammates or even herself to easily dispatch them with AoE damage. Star can also bend the rules of the poison mist by granting a temporary poison mist immune area centered around herself or a teammate.<br>
            Star excels in the mid-game of a match where poison mist can be threatening and her special action duration is relatively manageable. Though her effectiveness with special action falls at the later phases, she can still put opponents in very uncomfortable situations through her base kit.
        </p>

<h2 id="Alpha">Alpha Skill - Diffusion Laser</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch114/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch114_Unique1.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Star throws a laser that does damage on impact and upon contact explodes into a damaging AoE. This move can pierce enemies causing enemies hit to cause their own explosions. This damage can "echo" to other enemies when multiple enemies are pierced. It also boasts excellent range.

Star's Alpha is unique from most beamer projectiles in that it can be held by holding the fire button to have it ready to be thrown when released. Holding her Alpha will briefly pause the reload timer until the laser is created. It can also be rolled out of at any point to preserve ammo.

Enemies can dodge the laser, but the splash can still occur and hit other enemies. This means the enemy that is directly hit by Alpha will not receive damage, but other enemies nearby will be hit by the explosion damage that comes from Alpha.

At base damage, Star's level 9 Alpha does 20 damage on impact and 65 damage on splash. Totaling 85 damage on direct hit of Alpha. <br>
Accounting for the Strike passive she receives, it does 21 damage on impact and 68.25 damage on splash. Totaling 89.25 (Rounded to 90) damage on direct hit of Alpha.

Despite it's high base damage, it's attack speed is the lowest in it's class of beamer projectiles.

Tips for Star's Alpha:

- When dealing with an enemy that likes to spam roll, hold Alpha and wait for the recovery of the roll to punish.
- Mix in rolling and sprint jumping with Alpha to keep yourself mobile and unpredictable. Because Star's Alpha is roll-able, she can immediately roll after the Alpha projectile is thrown out.

<h2 id="Beta">Beta Skill - Zero Air</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch114/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch114_Unique2.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Star summons a vacuuming sphere of air in the target direction. It can be tapped to instantly place the sphere in-front of her or held to move the sphere forward in a fixed direction. Enemies caught within the sphere will be pulled to the center for 3 seconds. This strong vacuuming effect deals no damage and has no down power. Star is also slightly lifted up in the air while she is using Beta.

The sphere dramatically grows in size at level 4 and level 9, and it goes slightly down in cooldown at nearly every level.

Beta is a strong playmaking skill due to it's vacuuming effect. It can force enemies to take AoEs attacks from your teammates, keep them in place for your team to beam or start a combo, cover an opponent's wake-up escape, or even extend combos for teammates. The possibilities are endless.

Despite it's modest cooldown, the move takes time to setup, and Star is stationary until she completes the formation of the Sphere.

- Enemies on the floor can roll during Star's Beta, but if they're in the air they cannot. This turns the move into a combo/true damage tool.

<h2 id="Gamma"> Gamma Skill - Keraunos</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch114/GUI/Skill/T_ui_Skill_Ch114_Unique3.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Star performs a long-distance dashing uppercut that grabs enemies and launches them into the air. A construct of herself then gathers energy into a laser that does continuous damage, followed by a large hit of damage at the end of the grab. The grab lasts 3 seconds, and Star gets an incredible amount of damage and knockdown resistance, making it hard to stop her after a successful grab.

She starts with two charges of this move and gets a third at level 4. The reload time is six seconds per charge, and is shortened to five seconds per charge at level 9.

For 21 ticks, every tick of damage from Gamma does 3 damage with only one down power. Star is also capable of grabbing multiple opponents that are at point blank range of each other. This combined with no damage scaling for teammates makes it a powerful tool for team combos.

This move is also her main long-range movement tool, having a good bit of range, while also letting you keep a bit of momentum, similar to Present Mic or Kaminari.

Despite it's strengths, Gamma's tracking is inconsistent either hitting perfectly or missing completely. It also has a large amount of start-up, which is very react-able, and a large amount of recovery when whiffed, which can lead to a strong punish for many characters.

<h2 id="Special_Action"> Special Action - Invert Area</h2>
<p align="center">
    <img src="https://ultrarumble.com/assets/Character/Ch114/GUI/Skill/T_ui_SpecialSkill_Ch114.png" width="400" /></br>
</p>

Star raises her arm and uses New Order to invert the area around her or an ally turning any poison mist into a healing area. To Invert an area for an ally, aim down sights and use special action while looking at them.<br>
The person with Invert Area must be within the converted poison mist in order to receive healing. This goes for allies nearby as well. <br>
While Star is downed, she can use her special action on herself, but not allies.

Invert Area is also nicknamed "New Order" after her quirk.

Use time values were measured via recordings. Use times via https://ultrarumble.com/supply-items are not up to date.

<h2 id="Basic_Attacks">Basic Attacks</h2>

<h3 id="Ground_Melee">Ground Melee</h3>
Star performs a lunging punch, then performs a two hit punch.<br>
This move is good for quick and close-ranged punishes, where Gamma and Air would be too slow.

Melee 1 has decent tracking, but below average range.

Melee 2 has good tracking, but very little range.

Melee 2 can whiff at certain ranges and when hitting characters at certain heights due to Melee 1's knockback.

<h3 id="Air_Melee">Air Melee</h3>
Star dashes forward in the air and performs a swiping punch.<br>
This move is for mid-ranged punishes, where Gamma would be too slow and Ground Melee would be too short.

Air Melee has terrific tracking making it very good for proccing melee tuning, but is on the slower side and has relatively long recovery. The melee is difficult to combo into without assistance from an Air Beta or teammate.
Due to the melee performing a dash, it can be used to traverse in the air without using Gamma. It can be used to air stall, but has diminishing height gain.

<h3 id="Alpha-Ledge-Cancel">Alpha Ledge Cancel</h3>
Due Star being able to roll Alpha, you can roll after Alpha is thrown to cancel the recovery period when you move off a ledge. This allows you to perform any air action immediately after using Alpha.

<h3 id="Alpha-Roll-Cancel">Alpha Roll Cancel</h3>
At a specific timing, rolling after Alpha can be cancelled into another action. The action can be any button press as long as a movement key is held. This allows Star to do untrue combo follow-ups from her Alpha.
This tech is much easier on Keyboard and Mouse, but is possible on controller.

When Alpha is double-tapped and roll is pressed at the timing, the recovery of Alpha and roll is instantly cancelled. This functionally doesn't do anything different that Alpha cancels does already, but is kind of cool.

From the front, this is visually the exact frame where roll should occur. This is not the timing where you start to press the button, but the one when it needs to be pressed down.

<p align="center">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HydrosPlays/ultrarumbleguide/refs/heads/main/gifs/1100y1-gif.gif" width="400" />
</p>

<h4>FPS Manipulation</h4>
The cancel's time window is dependent on the frames per second of your game. On console, this is generally 60 fps minus Switch and on PC, this is more variable.
The lower the FPS, the easier the tech is to perform, with 30 fps being the easiest.
At higher FPS, you need to delay your timing slightly, the higher your FPS, the more you need to delay.

<h2>Strategy</h2>

<h3>Beta for Wake-ups</h3>

<h3>Deadman's Lightning Coil</h3>
A downed enemy can be used as bait to setup a double Alpha pierce. When you down an enemy, teammates will generally try to save their ally. Once they get close to their ally, place beta to pull both of them and begin raining down Alphas on their downed ally.

The aim for this is to at worst case finish off their ally and at best case down another enemy.

This strategy can be countered by enemies with shields or place-able terrain. If you know they have either, then it's best to simply finish off their teammate.

<p align="center">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HydrosPlays/ultrarumbleguide/refs/heads/main/gifs/1100y1-gif.gif" width="400" /><br>
</p>

<h3>Puppet on Strings</h3>
Catching an enemy in air beta is one of the most deadly situations to be placed in for most of the cast. The enemy is rendered completely helpless until Beta is finished and they land on the ground. Use this opportunity to get in free damage with Alphas, lock them down further with Gamma, or tack on damage with Alpha then Gamma while they're still helpless in the air.

<h4>Jump Pad</h4>
Jump pad is the easiest way to setup Air Beta and the most deadliest. The velocity of jump pad is too low to counteract the suction of Beta.

Pulling enemies off of ledges is the 2nd most deadliest way to setup a death trap. Most of the cast will be pulled helplessly into the air. However, characters that can build structures can cover the center of Beta.

<h3>Storm Sickness Resets</h3>
A key part of Star's game plan is to use New Order to reset the poison sickness. This greatly extends the time Star can be inside storm.<br>
Special action should be used to either heal to the large HP Recovery threshold or to reset poison sickness.

Once health is fully restored, the sequence for cycling healing is as follows:

- Big Green -> New Order toggle -> Repeat

Tips:

- While healing, it is a good idea to use reload cards in order to gain even more meter during the downtime.
- If more Special Action meter is required but you're still very healthy, you can use small HP recoveries to recover more meter. This becomes more unfeasible the longer the game goes.
- Refer to <a href="https://ultrarumble.com/guide/poison-mist">this guide</a> for the HP thresholds to drink a large recovery.

<h3>Teamplay</h3>

<h4>Beta</h4>
Beta can be used as a defensive deterrent for enemies that are chasing your teammate as it will keep them in place for 3 seconds. If all else fails, sheer damage can be the best way to stop an enemy.<br>
Beta can also be used to setup team combos or even extend team combos or depending on the situation.

<h4>Gamma</h4>
Gamma is a good option to keep enemies locked down, especially if they are distracted by your teammate. In rare cases, Gamma's laser can prevent entry in doorways because if an enemy comes in contact with the laser, they are vacuumed into the grab with their teammate.

<h4>Special Action</h4>
An incredibly underutilized portion of Star's kit is her Special action on teammates. Like for herself, Star can use Special Action to help her teammates recover in storm or extend their time in storm by resetting storm sickness.<br>
Star's special action has infinite range, so you can easily help teammates that are deep in storm.<br>
You must keep in mind both the positioning of your teammate, amount of Special Action you have, and the phase you are in.

<h2>Team Combos</h2>

Star's kit allows for nasty team combos. This section does not cover specific moves that combo into each other, but rather focuses their properties to generalize their usage.

### Team Combo Theory

#### Forced Ground Bounce

Forced Ground Bounce states in the air cannot be recovered from until they hit the ground or reach the down power threshold.

Star's Beta can extend the air-time of a ground bounce for up to 3 seconds.<br>
This allows for potentially extreme damage before an enemy is knocked down.

#### Air melee follow-ups

Star's air melee does a fairly long duration launch on HP. This opportunity can be used by teammates to follow-up off your attack.

#### Star's Gamma as a combo starter

Although hard to hit, Star's Gamma is one of few grabs that have no team damage scaling. This means while an enemy is grabbed by Star, her teammates can fire at them for 100% damage.

#### Grab Chains

Whether from a teammate's melee grab or a tether-style grab, Star can Gamma as a follow-up for incredible burst damage.

Generally the type of finisher on a grab is the most important part of a grab. Depending on your teammate's build and character, Star's grab can be worse or better than theirs.<br>
A general rule is if an enemy has GP, HP finishers take priority. If they are on HP, the highest damage ender takes priority. Star's grab has very low continuous damage, but a very high damage finisher.

<h2 id="Special_Tunes">Special Tunes</h2>

<h3>Tuning categorizations</h3>
<span class="difficulty-badge very-favorable">Highly Versatile 🧰</span><br>
These tunings can be used a plethora of situations, synergizes extremely well with Star's kit, or can potentially change how Star plays.

<span class="difficulty-badge even">Personal-Preference 🛠️</span><br>
These tunings have good uses when needed and are ultimately up to a player's own individual playstyle.

<span class="difficulty-badge very-unfavorable">Niche 🔨</span><br>
These tunings have rare use cases and are often used in specific playstyles or gimmicks.
