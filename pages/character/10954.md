# Season 1
## September 29th 2023 (or earlier)
### Character Datamines

As you can see, characters like Hawks, Kurogiri and Monoma were unchanged class/role-wise, but some characters had different roles.
- Shota Aizawa was a support.
- Overhaul was a strike.
- Present Mic was a technical.
- Nejire Hado was a strike.
- Hitoshi Shinso was a technical.
- Endeavor, Twice, All For One and Mirio's roles were not yet known.

We also got to see Shota Aizawa and Endeavor's 3D models, skill thumbnails and some of their costumes, along with costumes for released characters such as Denki Kaminari's Casual Wear and Eijiro Kirishima's Unbreakable. Another curious fact is that even though all of these emblems are identical to the ones we have now, Shinso is the only exception, as it looks like it was planned for his default costume to be his UA Track Suit, but then it was instead made a PUR and switching by his current Hero Costume for his release in Season 10.

### Stage Datamines
Even though it released in Season 3, UA Island (known as St02 internally) was found in Season 1's files, and we can actually see how the stage was structured through its datamined minimap, which you will notice is quite different from the current in some places.

<div align="center">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HydrosPlays/ultrarumbleguide/refs/heads/main/images/st02_leak1.png" width=50%/>
</div>

As you can see, while Destruction Area and Nature Area looked pretty much the same, the north and the center of the map were quite different, with UA being pushed to the north of the stage instead of being its center, which was occupied by other buildings.
