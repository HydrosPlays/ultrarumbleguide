Costume Lines are series of costumes that follow a common motif. Costumes are given different IDs according to the "category" and "line" they belong to. Currently, the following exist in My Hero Ultra Rumble:


(Default Line / 000 0)
(Maskless Line / 000 1)

## Default Category ("Default")
### Default Line (000 0)
This is where everyone's base costumes belong to, and by extent, this makes it the most populated line, with 100% of the roster having a costume in this line. There currently are 38 unique costumes in this line, or 190 if we count color variations.

## Equipment Category ("Eq")
The Equipment Costume category (also known as Category 0) contains costumes that, for the most part, are variations, upgrades, or downgrades of their default costumes, which also entails old and future versions of their costumes.

### Alternate Line 1 (000 1) (EqA1)
This is the first of the Alternate lines, and it's mostly populated by Mask Off variations of the character's default costume. Since not all characters wear a headpiece, their Alternate Line 1 costume is usually a previous version of their current costume, for example, Dabi's villain costume has no headpiece, so instead this slot is used for Dabi's beta villain costume (an older variation).<br>
<br>
Eijiro Kirishima's mask off costume does not belong to this category, likely because this costume has the Mask Off on his alpha costume rather than his default costume. Mr. Compress has entries that show his Alternative 1 costume exists, but isn't currently in the game. The fact that Mr. Compress's costume lacks a "Dangerous" color variation does shed some light on the matter, as in the times of Ultra Rumble's closed beta test in 2022 there were no Dangerous color variations, as Fancy was the last one. The Open Beta does not contain images of this costume. Izuku Midoriya has had his Beta Costume in the game's beta, where it was the default costume, however, if it released, it might be in this line.
