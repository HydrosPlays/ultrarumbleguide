## Datamined Characters
### Ch000

### Shoto Todoroki Phosphor? (Ch204)
In Season 16, a new character ID was leaked, that being Ch204. Normally, if there are no mentions of who this character could be, dataminers try to replace an existing character's skeleton by the new character one to see how much their model deforms and hopefully be able to guess who we're looking at. The bones for Ch204 match Shoto Todoroki's model perfectly, just like how OFA's fits base Deku's perfectly. # Datamined Gamemodes

## Orb Battle
Orb Battle is an apparently discontinued (or postponed) gamemode with mentions tracing back as early as the My Hero Ultra Rumble Open Beta Test. In the game's files it was found that there were objects called "Orbs" that look like small pink spheres, and there's also a file that dictates orb coordinates, the same type of file that lists Kota's spawns and natural civilian spawns. A Character Condition called Orb Penalty was also found.<br>

This mode used the base mode map (unlike Area Control), and the goal was apparently to get as many orbs as possible in a certain amount of time, as the description for the orbs mentions that the team with the most orbs wins. Plus Ultra stones (the item that looks like a star) are likely tied to this mode too, serving as a way to achieve Plus Ultra, or at the very least make it more frequent. The Orb Penalty condition could perhaps be a way to hinder the top teams as a "reverse comeback mechanic" to make sure there's always a small chance of winning even when the situation is rough.

## Scout Battle
Some speculate Scout Battle and Orb Battle are the same gamemode, however, Scout is quite a strange mode. Little is known about it, as references to it are scarce, but there are references to "Requesting a Scout", "Request to Loser", "Receive from Winner", "Replied to Winner" and "Make a Sidekick". Which could indicate it's a mode where you could make a sidekick for yourself and request scouts to players you defeated (or maybe NPCs...)

## Leaders Battle
Leaders Battle (formerly known as Kings Battle) is probably the gamemode that got the closest to be released, having significant internal updates around Seasons 5-7, however, this mode never released, either because it got canceled or delayed.<br>
<br>
The goal of this mode is to get the highest amount of points before the timer runs out (similar to Area Control), killing players gives 3 points, while killing leaders gives 10 and presumably instakills the whole team (though there seems to be a respawn functionality). When a match starts, a player in the team is made the "Leader", and this position will rotate across team members periodically. When the timer is close to hit zero, the whole party will gain the "Leader" status, which will make endkills much more significant. There's also mentions of a special variation of large item boxes that opens slightly faster and drops a lot of Ability Cards.<br>
<br>
This mode is technically already playable, it has all the data needed to play it, so it could resurface at some point... or not.

# Tuning Datamines

## GP Defense+
The Assault counterpart of the normal tuning effect GP Attack Power+. GP Defense+ is currently unused, as no character carries this effect currently. This normal tune decreases the damage received while having GP by the same values as HP Defense+.

## PU/PC Active Duration+
This is not Assault Endeavor's special tune, this is a normal tuning effect that increases Plus Ultra duration by 0.8s (level 3) or 1s (level 4). Currently no character carries this effect, but perhaps in the future we might see this interesting effect.
